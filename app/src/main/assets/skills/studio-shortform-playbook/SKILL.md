# Short-form playbook

## Hook (first 2-3 s decide everything)
- Frame 1 already has visual interest and movement; text appears within 0.5 s; voice starts at once; no logo intro, no "hey guys".
- Hook formulas: stake ("I was paying $19 a month for rectangles"), contradiction ("53 rendered. 2 matched."), failure ("The AI said done. It wasn't."), numbers with a twist, a command naming the thing, a visible demonstration (one word changes in the address bar). Write 3 and pick the most truthful.
- the owner's frame: I (a student) had this problem, I used AI, I checked its work, here is what broke and what I fixed. Problem first, product second ("I own BingwaOne which does X. But Y. So I built Tricid.").

## Voice and sarcasm
Dry, self-aimed ("Not the smart thing. The sensible thing."), AI-aimed ("It said done. It was not done."), situation-aimed ("Eight gigabytes. And a lot of ambition."). Never at people or the old service. One laugh line per 15-20 s, then a straight fact. Short sentences, spoken rhythm, numbers as spoken ("fifty-three"). Honest "still broken" line in every episode. Avoid code words; if one is needed, give the picture.

## Structure
Hook (stake) -> one line of credibility -> the story beats (each ONE idea, own shot) -> the turn/twist -> proof -> honest limit -> short close that calls back to the hook visual. If there is a list, show all items early as an open loop. End on the last word + 0.5-1 s; no long end card (<= 1.4 s). CTA only if asked; no comment-bait unless the owner wants it.

## Length and pace
30-45 s sweet spot (watch time beats length: 45 s at 70% completion beats 15 s at 40%). 90-110 spoken words for 35 s (4.0-4.7 words/s tight, ~2.7 w/s relaxed explainer). Shot average 1.1-1.5 s (reel style) or 2-3 s (explainer) with something moving in every shot. No dead air over 0.2-0.4 s.

## Layout and safe zones (1080x1920)
Universal safe area 900x1400 centred; bottom 300-380 px is platform UI: nothing important there. Captions at y ~1310 (the owner brand pill) never over a face/mouth, never white on light.

## Captions
Mandatory (80% watch muted; +12% retention). 1-3 words per chunk on phrase boundaries (Tricid pill style allows up to ~22 chars), appear 2 frames before the word, one accent word, one look for the whole film. Verify caption text equals the spoken words.

## B-roll order (video beats images)
1 real footage/screen recording of the thing (zoomed, cursor visible), 2 animated UI or poster output, 3 vector motion graphic metaphors, 4 AI video only if asked, 5 short highlight crops. Never invent numbers or results; crop to the phrase; zoom UI to fill the panel (small UI floating in empty space is the "AI edit" tell).

## Sound
Music by mood, bed 9-14 dB under voice, ducked under speech; SFX only on visible actions (soft taps, dry clicks, paper, air whoosh on real spatial moves), 10-20 ms audio lead; avoid lasers/plops. Vary files for repeated items. Master -14 LUFS, true peak <= -1.5 dBTP (measure on the finished mp4). I cannot hear: give a listen-check list.

## Formats worth reusing (see studio-style-bank)
Whiteboard/chalk explainer, creator kinetic type, software walkthrough with real captures, data story, illustrated "what happens if", paper/atlas history, stick-figure "what would you do", product launch film.
